# Cheat-sheet: the six parts and the methods

Brief and general. Evidence labels use the rubric: Strong, Some, Mixed, Practice only. Most methods here are widely used with little direct testing, and the labels say so. If a method library is present, prefer its card.

## The six parts by tool the user has

| Part | Paper | Phone notes | Calendar app | Task app | Spreadsheet |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Capture (one inbox) | A pocket notebook, page one | A note called Inbox | Not a good inbox | The app's inbox | A tab called Inbox |
| Organise | One page "Next actions", one "Projects" | Two notes with those names | Dated items only | Lists or projects, few | Two tabs |
| Plan | Today's page, three lines | Note with today's date | Time blocks | Today view | A Today row |
| Review | Walk the pages weekly | Walk the notes | Look one week ahead and back | Inbox zero, check lists | Sort and prune |
| Focus | Phone in a bag | Do not disturb on | Blocks marked busy | Hide all but Today | Not needed |
| Life | A page "Home" | A note "Life" | Family and health events | A Life project | A Life tab |

Rule: if two places do one job, pick one.

## Methods (name, what it does, evidence)

- **Single inbox:** everything is captured in one place first, sorted later. Practice only.
- **Next-action list:** every project has one visible next physical step. Practice only.
- **Weekly review:** a set time to empty the inbox, check calendar and lists, pick next week's outcomes. Practice only.
- **Daily plan with Most Important Tasks (1 to 3):** choose the few things that make the day a success before starting. Practice only.
- **Time blocking:** put tasks in calendar slots, with buffers. Mixed; works for some people and fails when days are unpredictable.
- **If-then planning (when X, I will Y):** attach an action to a trigger. Some; research on implementation intentions supports it for goal follow-through.
- **Shutdown routine:** a short end-of-day list check and a stop line. Practice only.
- **Done list:** write what was finished so progress is visible. Practice only.
- **Distraction parking lot:** note stray thoughts during focus instead of acting on them. Practice only.

Start with the first four for most people. Add one more only after a week of using them.

## Default time budget

About 15 minutes a day, 45 to 60 a week, 20 a month. Say if the user's budget is different.

## Typical cuts

Too many places: merge. Too many tags or colours: drop them. A task app that is not opened: go back to paper or notes. A review that always slips: shrink it to 15 minutes at a fixed time with a trigger.
