# Setup Checklist: day 1 to day 7

*One small job a day, none over 30 minutes. If you miss a day, do it the next day; do not catch up on everything. Cross out parts you do not need.*

| Day | Job | Minutes | Done |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| 1 | **Pick the inbox.** Name the one place everything goes (a notebook, a note, an app). Put it where you will see it. Empty your head into it: everything you are carrying. | 20 | [ ] |
| 2 | **Make the lists.** Create "Next actions" and "Projects". Move items from the inbox: each gets a next step or is deleted. | 30 | [ ] |
| 3 | **Set up the plan.** Choose where the daily plan lives. Write today's up to three must-dos. Put fixed commitments (work, family, health) on the calendar. | 20 | [ ] |
| 4 | **Add the focus rules.** Pick two. Try one real work block with the phone away and notifications off. | 25 | [ ] |
| 5 | **Add the life place.** Create the "Life" page or list. Add this week's personal commitments and one protected anchor. | 15 | [ ] |
| 6 | **Dry-run the weekly review.** Use the steps: empty the inbox, check next week's calendar, scan the lists, choose three outcomes for the week. Set a fixed slot for it. | 30 | [ ] |
| 7 | **Day-7 review.** Answer below. Update `my-system.md` and the trial log. | 15 | [ ] |

## Day-7 review

1. Did I use the inbox every day? [yes / mostly / no]
2. What took longer than the work it organised? [ ]

4. What dropped less this week than before? [ ]
