**Reading the columns**

- **Job it does.** One phrase. Not the vendor's category, what it is actually used for.
- **Cost.** What is actually billed: seats paid times price, or the usage-tier amount, not the plan's sticker price.
- **Last used.** The user's own estimate, or "not known" if they do not have one. Never a guess written as if it were a fact.
- **Overlaps with.** Every other row whose "job it does" phrase matches this one.
- **Verdict.** Exactly one of keep, cancel, downgrade, consolidate. See `SKILL.md` Step 4 for what each means.
- **Reason.** One line. A verdict with no reason is a guess written down.

**Exceptions.** A tool correctly kept despite low use, such as a disaster-recovery or compliance tool, gets a reason that says so explicitly rather than a blank that looks like an oversight.
