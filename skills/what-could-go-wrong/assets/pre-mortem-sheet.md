**Group** is internal, external, people or assumption. All four should appear.

**Likely? Damage?** is words only: likely or unlikely, survivable or fatal, plus a clause of reasoning. No percentages.

**Early-warning signal** is something a person could see in a calendar, tracker, inbox or number, with a date.

**If-then response** names the action and who takes it, so nobody has to call a meeting to decide whether to act.
