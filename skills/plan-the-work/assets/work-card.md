## The 5-line card

For work of up to about two hours. Keep five lines only.

```
Outcome:   [one observable sentence]
Done:      [2 or 3 checkable criteria, separated by semicolons]
Out:       [what this does not include]
First move: [one action, under 30 minutes, with a time if known]
Assumed:   [the guesses, and what could not be checked]
```

## When to skip the card

- The task is under about 30 minutes: give the first move only.
- Outcome, audience, done condition and deadline are all already written down: say so, then add only the risk and the first move.
