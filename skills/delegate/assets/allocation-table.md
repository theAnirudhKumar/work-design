**Goes to** is the user, a named person, or a model. Nothing else.

**The check** is a specific thing to look at, not an instruction to be careful. "Confirm every figure in the summary appears in the source" is a check. "Review it" is not.

**Take it back if** is a threshold, not a feeling. Two errors found, any error in a number, any claim without a source.
